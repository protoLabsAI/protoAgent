Control the browser — silent recording.

1. Open Browser after enabling Agent Browser.
2. Enter https://example.org and choose Go; wait for the live page.
3. Manually change the URL to https://example.com and choose Go.
4. Return to Chat, open a new chat, and ask browser_screenshot to capture the current page without navigating.
5. The actual tool saves a PNG under the isolated instance capture directory. The recording shows its returned path, not a downloaded local copy.

No logins, forms, or private websites are used. The public example pages may change.
