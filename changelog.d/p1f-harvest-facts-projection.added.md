- **Conversations in Work — public-only harvest, archives and facts.** Refs #5422. Phase 1 proof slice P1f.2 (ADR 0131 §5, amended by bd-jo3v6). When a conversation that had private operator steering is harvested, the summary, compaction archive, closed-chat digest card and extracted facts are now built only from its **public** part: private-partition messages and non-public archive spans are dropped at a single input boundary (`graph.conversation_audience.public_projection`), so a later ordinary turn that clears `incognito` cannot re-expose retained private material. Summaries, archives, closed cards and facts record an audience and projection source-version; private archive spans are kept out of searchable shared history; and fact extraction stays off for unknown or mixed provenance, with public source lineage carried into storage and dedup so a private fact never merges with or supersedes a shared one. Dormant mechanism exercised only by tests — no user-reachable surface, switch or migration; behaviour is unchanged for conversations without a private partition. Builds on #5403/#5421; the extractor boundary is kept for the ADR 0125 memory epic (#4555) Phase 2–3.
