- **The nightly eval gate reports how often unattended work interrupted the operator.** `scripts/evals_nightly.py` now counts each source's Activity cards and Needs-you items (scheduler, inbox, watch, background) over the 24 hours before the run, prints them as a per-source table and writes them into the nightly report JSON under `interruptions`. A count above its daily threshold (defaults: 20 cards, 5 Needs-you items per source; change them with `--interruption-threshold [SOURCE:]KIND=LIMIT`) records one `evals-nightly` harness friction entry, `nightly gate regression: interruptions:<source>:<cards|needs_you>`, and fails the night. The first night the count is back under resolves it. `--dry-run` prints the counts and records nothing. Fixes #5518.
