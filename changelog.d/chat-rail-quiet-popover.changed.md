- **A quieter work-chip popover on the chat status rail (Refs docs/design/chat-status-rail-v2.md).** A Doing now / Coming up / Told it chip now opens a panel holding only its own group (chat-status-rail-v2 §1 D1) — the old Needs you and People riders are gone from work-chip panels (the Needs you marker and `+K` picker are always on the rail, so neither repeats inside). Each row is one composite button with the item text (wrapped, two-line clamped) over a single quiet line from `railCue(item, now)` — an age or next time, never a status word — and clicking it closes the panel and opens that item's Work detail. The heading reads "<group> · N" (the count quiet and decorative), the row list scrolls on its own while the heading and one "Open in Work" stay pinned, and a group that empties while its panel is open now holds a `role="status"` line ("Nothing running in this chat now.", etc.) instead of closing under the cursor. The old hard truncation and stray `meta` label are gone; tokens only, no design-system change.
