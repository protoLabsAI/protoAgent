- **A cancelled delegation's tool card can now be dismissed from the chat (#3095).** Stopping
  a running `task` delegation settles its card through the normal tool_end stream — same as
  any finished call — so it became permanent, fully-expandable history with no way out of the
  transcript's visual flow. The settled cancelled card (detected by the cancellation sentinel
  its result carries) now grows a small × in its header: dismissing hides the card in that
  client's view only, persisted locally so a reload doesn't resurrect it. No backend call is
  made — chat history keeps the full turn, and normal completed or failed tool calls render
  exactly as before (no new control).
