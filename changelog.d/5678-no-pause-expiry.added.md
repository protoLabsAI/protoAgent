- **Decisions without a paused turn expire and dismiss the way paused ones do.** When a decision that has no checkpoint pause reaches its deadline, its stated default is recorded and delivered to the agent as a new turn; one with no default simply expires and starts no turn. Dismissing such a decision closes the record without starting a turn. Paused decisions behave exactly as before. Dormant until no-pause decisions are switched on. Refs #5678.
