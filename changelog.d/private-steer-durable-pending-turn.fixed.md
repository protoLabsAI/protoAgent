- **Recover private steers after restart.** Private steering now durably records accepted turns before sending, tracks running and parked segments, and completes them with private replies. Failed, canceled, or closed executions release their reservations before the next private steer of the session may claim, and private steers run in the order they were accepted: an older one whose delivery was refused is started first instead of being left queued until a restart. Queued private steers recover through the private path; crash-interrupted running turns remain suspended without replay. The default-off setting and ordinary steering are unchanged. Refs #5504.
