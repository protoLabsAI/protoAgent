- **A watch-woken turn no longer mistakes its own wake for another one (#3494).** The scheduler deletes a one-shot job only after the turn it started returns, so `<working_state>` listed that job under PENDING SCHEDULES for the whole turn. An agent woken by a watch reaction read it as a second queued wake and re-checked state it had already handled until the stall guard stopped it. The job that started the current turn is now marked `FIRING NOW`.
- **Harvested memories carry the date of the conversation, not the harvest (#3494).** The TTL sweep retires threads weeks after their last turn, and their summaries and extracted facts were stored undated, so an old "the user runs model X" was recalled as the current setup. Summaries and facts are now prefixed `[as of YYYY-MM-DD]` from the thread's last checkpoint.
