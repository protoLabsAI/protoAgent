- **With the chat open in two tabs, a reply is saved correctly again.** Since v0.248.0 the console saved a turn's task id the moment it arrived. A second tab took that as a turn it had to pick up and started its own requests for it while the first tab was still streaming, and its copy could replace the finished reply in the saved conversation. The task id is saved on the normal two-second timer again. This also brings back an older limit that v0.248.0 had removed: if you reload within two seconds of sending a message that then asks you a question, the question's form may not come back.
