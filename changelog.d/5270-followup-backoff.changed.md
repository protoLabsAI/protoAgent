- **A recurring follow-up backs off to hourly while its chat waits on you and nothing changes (#5270).** When a recurring scheduled job reports nothing new twice in a row and its origin chat is waiting on the operator (a `waiting` task or a pending decision), it now posts a single "Still waiting on you. Checking hourly until something changes." card and drops to one fire per hour — instead of re-reporting the same parked work every slot (one did so ~17 times in an afternoon). The backoff clears the moment a run has something new to say, the waiting task/decision resolves, or the job is edited; one-shot and `wait:` jobs never back off, and an unreadable store means no backoff (ADR 0129 S14).
