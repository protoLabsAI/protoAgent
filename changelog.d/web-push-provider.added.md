- **Web Push provider: notices can be handed to a device's push service without their text.** A notice due for a device with a push binding is now sent to that device's Apple, Google or Mozilla push service as a fixed, versioned payload (notice id, kind, importance, times, generic title and body, and a relative console route) encrypted for the browser's client keys (RFC 8291, `aes128gcm`) and signed with the VAPID key version the device enrolled under (RFC 8292), with the instance's public console origin as the VAPID subject. Notice text, links and credentials never leave the machine. The endpoint is validated again before every send and redirects are not followed; `404`/`410` retire the subscription, `429`/`5xx` and timeouts retry and honour `Retry-After`, and `401`/`403` end the attempt while keeping the binding. Nothing starts the sender yet (ADR 0135 W2). Refs #5539.
