- **A private steer stays out of traces, prompt snapshots and the fleet trace export.** Tracing, prompt capture and the fleet trace export already checked whether a conversation's content is public, but in production that check had no reader behind it, so every conversation read as public. It now reads the record the private-steer path writes: a conversation holding a private steer records structure, timing, usage and cost but no content, and is skipped by the fleet trace export, on the steered turn and on every later turn (it does not depend on the turn's incognito flag). A conversation with no private steer is traced, captured and exported exactly as before. If the record cannot be read, nothing is recorded rather than assuming the conversation is ordinary. Fixes #5403.
