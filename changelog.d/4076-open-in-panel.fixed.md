- **"Open in panel" no longer shows a blank or stale Artifact panel (#4076).** Opening the panel from a chat chip rendered the artifact the panel last remembered and then, about 2ms later, the one you clicked. Chrome drops a frame swap made while the previous one is still loading, so the panel kept the old artifact, or stayed black, under the correct picker label until a reload. The panel now resolves the clicked artifact before it renders, and any swap requested mid-load waits for that load and then shows the newest one.
