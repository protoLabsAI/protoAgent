- **A goal's turn now ends once its verifier passes, and "Goal set." is printed once (#4012).**
  A goal started with `/goal new` used to keep running after the work was done. The agent
  fixed the bug and saw the tests pass, then re-explored the repo and said "the goal is
  already complete" two or three more times before the turn ended. In the chat this looked
  like several runs stacked in one reply. Now, after each tool step of a goal turn, the
  goal's check (command, test, CI, data or plugin; never the LLM judge) runs, and the turn
  stops as soon as it passes. Checks are spaced out: at most one per step, and at least 10
  seconds (or twice the last check's duration) apart. A "checking the goal…" status shows
  while one runs. The goal's set confirmation, in chat and in the Goals and Work panel
  toasts, no longer says "Goal set." twice.
