- **Org Chart view's Refresh and panel Close buttons now use the DS plugin-kit button (#551).** Both action buttons were hand-rolled `<button>`s with local CSS; they now carry the kit's `pl-btn` classes (`pl-btn--sm` for Refresh, `pl-btn--ghost pl-btn--icon pl-btn--sm` for Close), gaining `type="button"` and, for Close, `aria-label="Close"`. The local `.refresh`/`.close` rules are dropped (only the Close float layout stays); no literal colours or sizes are introduced and the click behaviour is unchanged.
