- **Conversations in Work keeps private steering out of traces and prompt captures.** When a turn reads a conversation's private partition, tracing and prompt capture now record only content-free structure, timing and usage — no prompts, arguments, results or errors — across nested, background and terminal spans, and a later ordinary turn cannot clear the suppression to re-expose retained private material. Routed through the one shared public-source boundary and dormant until that boundary is bound, so tracing and capture are unchanged for conversations with no private partition.
