- **An agent can quote a past session's whole final answer.** `recall_session(session_id, entry="final")` returns the session's final answer in full (reasoning stripped and credentials redacted, as the search index does), capped at about 12,000 characters with a truncation marker. The session card now keeps up to 12,000 characters of the final answer instead of 2,000 and flags one it had to clip. The summary view says when its final answer is only an excerpt, and an unknown `entry` id now lists the session's real entry ids and points at `entry="final"` instead of ending in "No history entry found".
