- **Thread-safe embedding breaker and audit session stats (#4201).** The knowledge store's embedding circuit breaker (reached concurrently by `asyncio.to_thread` searches, the `warm_probe` daemon thread and the operator reset) and the audit logger's in-memory per-session stats (written from both the event loop and worker threads) now serialize their state changes, so a concurrent failure count can't be lost, the breaker can't be left half-open, and a session's stats can't drop a first write, raise a KeyError during eviction, or report a torn snapshot.
