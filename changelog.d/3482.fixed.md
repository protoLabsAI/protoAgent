- **Frozen desktop servers keep their heartbeats (#3482).** The pid-reuse guard behind the `.instances/` heartbeats only recognised `python -m server`, so the desktop app's `protoagent-server` sidecars — and a `protoagent serve` from `uv tool install` — were pruned as recycled pids on every runtime-status poll: six servers running, an empty `.instances/`, no colocation warning, and nothing discoverable by heartbeat. The guard now also accepts the frozen sidecar and console-script binary names.
