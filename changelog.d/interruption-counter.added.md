- **Evals count how often unattended work interrupts the operator.** `evals/interruptions.py` reports, for the 24 hours ending at a given time, the Activity cards posted and the Needs-you items raised by each source: scheduler, inbox, watch and background. It reads the instance's own Activity log, scheduler run history, background job ledger, inbox and decisions ledger, read-only. A scheduled run that stayed quiet (`NO_UPDATE`, or a repeated result) is not counted as a card. Operator-chat decisions are excluded from unattended-source counts, and inbox alerts count only operator-class priority-now items, not exhausted delivery retries alone. `python -m evals.interruptions` prints the counts. Refs #5518.
