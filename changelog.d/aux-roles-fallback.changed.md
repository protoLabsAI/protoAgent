- **Auxiliary model calls fail over on a flaky provider.** Conversation harvest, fact extraction and on-demand compaction now build their model through the ADR 0133 role chain (`create_role_llm`) instead of a single aux model: when the role resolves to more than one model (e.g. `routing.aux_model: [primary, backup]`), a retryable provider error — a 429, a 5xx, a connection timeout — retries the next model in the chain instead of failing the whole pass. A single-string `routing.aux_model` (today's default) is a one-entry chain and behaves exactly as before. Harvest and facts retain their historical aux selection: the reserved `memory.harvest_model` and `memory.facts_model` overrides remain inactive at these call sites. With aux blank, roles inherit the full main chain, including legacy `routing.fallback_models`.
