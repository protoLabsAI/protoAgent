- **Steering a peer's thread no longer locks the peer out of it.** When you steered an idle conversation that another agent started over federation, your turn was saved as yours inside their thread, and the peer's next message into its own conversation was refused with "Context … is not available to this caller". A thread now belongs to whoever started it: you can add turns, the peer can still continue it, and the peer still can't read your turns (#5379).
