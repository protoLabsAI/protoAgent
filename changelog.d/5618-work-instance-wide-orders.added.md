- **Instance-wide standing orders show in Work → Your instructions, in every chat.** An order that applies to every chat and every unattended turn is listed first, tagged "Instance-wide", and counts in each chat's instructions count and rail chip. Its detail says where it applies, where and when you said it (with "(your time)" in the text), lets you edit it, and withdraws it everywhere with an optional reason. Cancelling a withdraw now returns keyboard focus to Withdraw…, and after a withdraw focus moves to the next instruction instead of being lost. Long instructions no longer make the Work list scroll sideways on a phone. Refs #5542.
