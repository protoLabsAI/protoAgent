- **Opening the console no longer replays every alert since your last visit.** A console that was closed or asleep catches up on the events it missed, and each one fired its toast and system notification again: goals achieved or failed, watches met or expired, background agents starting and finishing, scheduled results, resumed turns. Catch-up still happens, so chats and Work show what happened, but an event more than two minutes old no longer raises a toast or a notification.
