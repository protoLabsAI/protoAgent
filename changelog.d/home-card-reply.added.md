- **Reply on a Home card.** Alert, brief and scheduled-report cards in Home now have a "Reply" action. It quotes the entry into Home's composer, attaches a hidden reference so the agent knows which entry you mean, and focuses the composer. Delete the quote and the reference is dropped with it, so it never rides along on an unrelated message. The reply is an ordinary Home message: it never resumes or steers the job that raised the entry. Decision cards keep "Answer" and get no Reply. Refs #5545.
