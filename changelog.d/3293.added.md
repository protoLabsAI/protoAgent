- **Live knowledge search in the command palette.** Press ⌘⇧K, type two or more characters, and the palette now searches the agent's knowledge store — findings, notes, the daily log, harvested sessions — listing the top matches under a **Knowledge** heading; picking one opens Knowledge ▸ Store with that same search already run, so the entry you chose is in the list you land on. It stays quiet until you type (an empty box would otherwise list the store's most recent entries and bury the commands), shows a handful of matches rather than everything that matched, and says **Knowledge search unavailable** with the reason when a search fails or times out instead of quietly showing nothing.
