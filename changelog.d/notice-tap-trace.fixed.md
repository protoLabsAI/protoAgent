- **A tapped notification keeps being looked for after the console comes to the front, and Settings → Devices shows what happened to the tap.** On an installed phone console the tap could still land on the hub's chat: the phone can bring the console forward before the tap has been handled, and the console looked only once. It now keeps looking for ten seconds. "Notifications on this device" also gains a folded "What happened to the last notification tap" log with a Copy button, listing each step the notification handler and the console took (step names, notice ids and page paths only), so a wrong landing can be diagnosed from the phone.
