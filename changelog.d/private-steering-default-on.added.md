- **Private steering is on by default.** When you steer an agent inside a thread another agent or person started, and mark the steer private (`"private": true` on `POST /api/chat/sessions/{id}/steer`), the agent acts on it without your words or its private reply reaching that peer. Peer callers (the fleet token, paired agent devices, federation) can't send or read private steers. Ordinary chats and agent-to-agent traffic are unchanged. Set `private_steering.enabled: false` to turn it off. There's no console control for it yet.
