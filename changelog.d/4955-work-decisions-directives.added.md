- **Work view: Decisions and Directives sections (#4955).** The Work view gains its Decisions and Directives sections. Decisions lists the agent's "decision needed" ledger with pending items pinned first (as in the Needs you lane), each row showing the question, its status (pending → needs you, answered → done, expired/cancelled/withdrawn → cancelled), "Asked {time}" or "Answered: {label}", and the agent name only in the All chats filter; opening a row shows the existing DecisionCard unchanged. Directives renders the standing-instructions ledger — reusing the directive drawer's body — so an in-force instruction keeps the word "Active" under a neutral group, with history in its own collapsed group. No decision, thread or directive id is shown outside Technical details.
