- **A delegate's badge turns red when a dispatch to it fails.** A failed `delegate_to` send now degrades the same health record the background probe writes (source `dispatch`, with the error as the reason) and backs the next probe off the same way a failed probe does. Before, a delegate whose card answered the probe while every real send timed out stayed green in the console and in `list_agents`. The next successful probe or dispatch clears it; a cancelled dispatch does not count as a failure. Refs #5535 (ADR 0135).
