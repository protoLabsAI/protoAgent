- **A background job whose turn outlived the client wait is no longer marked failed.** When the background manager stopped waiting on a long detached turn (a read timeout or a dropped connection after its own `/a2a` had the request), it marked the job `failed` with a blank "Background turn delivery error: " cause, so the turn's real completion was ignored and its final report was lost. Now only a real delivery failure (connection refused, an error response) fails the job; otherwise the turn's own completion settles it, and a failure cause names the exception even when its message is empty. Fixes #5797
