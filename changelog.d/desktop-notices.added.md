- **Desktop app shows hub notices as native notifications.** While the desktop app runs, with its window open or closed to the tray, it reads the local hub's event stream and shows each notice (a decision that needs you, a scheduled result, a fleet-health or spend alert) as an OS notification with generic text. It reconnects with backoff and replays what was raised while disconnected, shows each notice once, and skips expired ones. In the desktop app the console leaves those alerts to the native side so they are not shown twice; browsers are unchanged. Refs #5539.
