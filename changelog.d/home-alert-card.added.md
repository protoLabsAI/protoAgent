- **Home shows briefs, alerts and decision pointers as one clear card.** A Home entry (a stopped fleet member, a scheduled result, a decision that waits) renders as a card with an icon, a one-line title, a neutral "Urgent" badge for high importance, the time, and a link to what raised it. A decision pointer has an "Answer" action that opens the decision. The card needs the Home conversation, which is behind the `home_conversation` developer flag. Refs #5545.
