- **Work, decisions, inbox/Feed and background jobs ride the durable change feed (#5196, ADR 0130 S5).** The Work view (`useWorkItems`), the fleet-wide decisions store (`useDecisions`), the chat's background-job store and the per-session directive ledger now treat the S1 durable change log as the source of truth: each surface still reacts to its own targeted bus topics (`decision.changed`, `task.changed`, `scheduler.changed`, `background.{started,completed}`, `inbox.item`/`activity.message`, `directive.changed`), which the server replays on reconnect from the client's cursor, and each now also re-reads on a `resync_required` cursor gap (D3). This retires the per-surface ad-hoc reconnect invalidations (the blanket `onConnectionChange` refetch each store carried) and the Work view's self-disabling 60s schedules poll — the feed covers both. The decisions pill and the Work "Needs you" rail read the same `useDecisions`, so their counts stay consistent. Adds an S0-style multi-client contract case: a decision raised or answered in one client appears in the other within 2s, with no reload or poll.
