- **The chat status rail is quieter: a still "Doing now" icon, and no work chips on desktop.**
  With no goal, the "Doing now" chip now shows a play icon that does not animate, so a tracked
  task with nothing running no longer looks like live work. On desktop the rail no longer shows
  the "Doing now", "Coming up", "Instructions" and "Needs you" chips or their details panels —
  notifications, the Work panel and the utility bar already carry that — so only the people
  chips and their picker remain, and with no people the rail takes no space above the composer.
  Mobile is unchanged: the chips and their swipe-up details stay as they were.
