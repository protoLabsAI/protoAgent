- **Tapping a push notification opens Home at that notice.** When Home holds an entry for the tapped notice, the console closes Settings, selects the Home chat and scrolls to that entry's card, including for a decision, whose card has the "Answer" action. A tap usually arrives before the next refresh, so the console reads Home's entries first if the transcript does not show the notice yet. Without a Home entry (or with the `home_conversation` developer flag off) a tap behaves as before: a decision opens its answer form, and any other notice follows the route the push worker named. Refs #5545, #5539.
