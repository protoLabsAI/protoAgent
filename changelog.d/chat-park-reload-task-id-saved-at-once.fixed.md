- **A reload in a turn's first seconds brings its question back.** The console now saves a turn's task id the moment it arrives instead of on the 2 s streaming timer, so reloading before that timer fires still reattaches to a turn parked on an `ask_human` question, form or approval and shows the form again. The tab that sent the message claims the turn while it streams and releases the claim once its finished state is saved. A second open tab leaves the turn alone until then, and adopts the sender's saved reply instead of reattaching and replacing it. Fixes #5514.
