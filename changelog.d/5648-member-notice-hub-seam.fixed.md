- **A decision a fleet member asks for now shows on every device the hub serves.** A member's notice handed to its hub is now raised through the hub's own notice path, so it reaches the desktop app's native banner and the hub's chat adapters as well as your phone. Before, only the phone push got it. The member's text and link still never leave the member: the hub shows "A notice from" and the member's name. Quiet hours and the push policy apply as before.
