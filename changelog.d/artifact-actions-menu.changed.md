- **Artifact panel actions always sit in the ⋯ menu.** Edit, Open in browser, Download and Delete now live in the toolbar's ⋯ menu at every panel width, the way a narrow dock or phone already showed them, so a wide panel's toolbar is just the picker, the version arrows, Links and ⋯. The menu behaves as before: picking an action closes it, the first Delete click asks "Confirm?", Escape closes it and returns focus to ⋯, and the arrow keys move between the actions.
