- **A "Standing instructions" view shows what the agent believes you've allowed it to do in a chat (#4861).** A quiet strip above the composer summarises the active instructions the agent re-reads every turn, and a drawer — opened from that strip, from a bare `/directives`, or from a chat tab's right-click menu — lists each one in your own words with the conditions it applies under, keeps a history of the ones that have closed, and lets you withdraw any of them with an optional reason. Withdrawing from here also leaves a quiet "You withdrew: …" note in the transcript so the change is visible where the agent reads.
