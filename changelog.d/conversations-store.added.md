- **Console data layer for Other conversations (#5283).** A new `apps/web/src/chat/conversations-store.ts` holds the agent's outside A2A threads for the forthcoming Conversations tab: it lists them through `chatApi.listConversations({ group, before, limit })` (`GET /api/conversations`), pages older 50 at a time with `before=`, and stays live over the bus's a2a `turn.started` / `turn.finished` events — updating a known row's running/needs-you status and refetching when an unknown context first starts, with a reconnect refetch and no polling. It also owns the tab's client-only UI state: whether this device has hidden it (per-agent localStorage, returning on its own when a thread needs you or a deep link opens), the derived `count` (7-day window), `running`, `needsYou` and `tabVisible`, and the stage (`chat` vs `conversations`) with the open thread, kept separate from the chat store's `currentSessionId`. The reserved stage id `conversations` is never sent to the server. Nothing is rendered yet. (docs/design/other-conversations.md §4.7, slice C1.)
