- **The Conversations tab reaches the phone, pinned at the top of the session sheet.** On a phone the pinned Conversations tab becomes an item above the chats in the session sheet — the `MessagesSquare` icon, "Conversations", the 7-day count, a non-pulsing status dot (warning when a thread needs you, else info when one is running) and a muted "Read-only", with no delete control. It appears and hides under the same rules as the desktop tab, and a long-press opens a "Hide Conversations tab" menu (matching the desktop right-click). Tapping it closes the sheet and shows the read-only list full screen, with the header reading "Conversations" where a chat title would be and the title tap still opening the sheet; tapping a thread opens it full screen, the header gaining a **← List** back, and the thread footer stacking the note over full-width **Continue in a new chat** and **Copy link**. The phone's chat screen gains nothing. (docs/design/other-conversations.md §3.7, slice C5.)
