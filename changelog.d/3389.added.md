- **Plugin setup gaps can carry a bounded, declarative remediation action (#3389).** `registry.report_setup_gap()` and `setup_gaps.report()` now accept an optional `action` hint or hint list, sanitize it to a closed `plugin_config`/`global_settings` vocabulary, and expose the structured gap records in runtime status alongside the unchanged legacy `warnings[]` strings. Unsafe, unknown, malformed, or oversized action payloads are omitted instead of crashing plugin loading.
