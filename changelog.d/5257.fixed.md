- **A stalled primary model no longer costs every call its full reconnect budget before the fallback runs (#5257).** When the primary fails as *unavailable* (a transport drop, an SDK timeout, a first-token stall; never our own in-flight queue timing out), `routing.fallback_models` now opens a process-wide breaker for that primary. For a cool-off (60 s, doubling per consecutive trip to 600 s), calls go straight to the fallbacks, and the first call after it expires probes the primary again. A success closes the breaker. When every fallback fails during the cool-off, the primary is still tried. A refusal (4xx, quota 429, context overflow) never trips it. Previously every call started on the dead primary and paid 3 × 120 s before falling back, so workflow stages with 300–450 s budgets never reached a healthy fallback. Vera completed no reviews for 4.5 h on 2026-11-13 while DeepSeek was up.
