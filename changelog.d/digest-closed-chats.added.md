- **Recently closed chats stay in the prior-sessions digest.** A chat closed with "Save a summary" (or harvested by the idle sweep) now keeps a slot in the digest, marked "closed", for `memory.closed_session_days` (default 14) — its gist there is the first sentence of its harvest summary. Erase, Shift+click and bulk close leave no closed entry, and Erase removes an existing one (ADR 0132 D3b).
