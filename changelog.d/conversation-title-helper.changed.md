- **Conversation titles drop a redundant "From <caller>:" prefix, and their meta line shows an absolute time.** A new pure `displayTitle(title, { caller, onBehalfOf })` helper trims a leading "From Claude Code (Josh's session): …" from a conversation title when the named caller is the one already shown on the origin line — matched literally, case-insensitively and on a word boundary, only within the first 60 characters, and never down to an empty title — and capitalises what remains. A shared `conversationMeta` formatter renders the muted line as "<origin without the leading 'From'> · 14 Nov, 12:29 · N messages" (count only above 1) with the long "From … · 14 Nov at 12:29 · N messages" form for its tooltip. Pure helpers only; the Conversations tab wires them in next. (docs/design/other-conversations.md §3.4, §9 C9.)
