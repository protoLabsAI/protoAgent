- **A pinned Conversations tab lists the agent's other conversations, read-only.** The chat tab bar now draws a pinned first **Conversations** tab as soon as the agent has outside A2A threads in the last 7 days — a badge with the count and a non-pulsing status dot (warning when a thread needs you, else info when one is running), its accessible name spelling out the count, the status in words and "read-only". Selecting it replaces the chat with a read-only list pane — a "For you" group, a collapsed "Automated · N" disclosure and "Show older", no composer — while leaving the open chat untouched; selecting a chat tab returns to it. Right-click the tab to hide it per device (it comes back on its own when a thread needs you, or via "Show Conversations tab" on the tab-bar background), and the pane carries loading, failed and automated-only states.
