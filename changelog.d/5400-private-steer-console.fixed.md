- **Replying from Work to a conversation another agent started now stays private.** When you answer a waiting job in Work whose source is a conversation listed under From others, or steer that conversation from the chat, your words now always go privately to your agent, and the peer that started the thread never reads them. There is no new switch: it is always private. If the server refuses the message, you see one plain sentence saying why it was not sent (the conversation is waiting on the other agent's own question, a private message can only be text, or private messages are off or need you to sign in again), and what you typed stays in the field. Known limit: your agent's own context still holds the text. Fixes #5400, Fixes #5401.
