- **Typing in the chat composer answers the open decision.** When an agent's decision card is waiting in a chat, a message typed in that chat's composer is sent as the answer; you no longer press "Reply in chat instead" first, and the message is no longer held behind the card. The composer's placeholder reads "Type your answer…" while the decision waits (screen readers hear "Your message answers {agent}'s question."), and nothing else about the composer changes. "Reply in chat instead" is gone from the card in the chat itself and stays on the Work detail, the Decisions panel and the Needs you rail. Approval gates and other forms still hold typed text, so a message can never approve a command, and a message starting with `/` still runs as a slash command. Refs #5668.
