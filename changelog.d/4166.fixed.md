- **Secret redaction no longer eats the quote, bracket or comma after a token (#4166).**
  The bearer pattern matched `\S+`, so `"Authorization: Bearer <jwt>",` reached the
  review panel as `"Authorization: Bearer [REDACTED]` and a PR was FAILed on a
  SyntaxError that wasn't on disk. Every pattern now replaces only the secret's own
  characters (and `github_pat_`, `npm_`, standalone JWTs and URL-userinfo passwords are
  covered too); code-review roles are told `[REDACTED]` marks are the runtime's, never
  a defect.
