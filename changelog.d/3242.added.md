- **Memory rows now say *when* they enter the prompt (ADR 0108 D4, #3242).** A new
  `delivery_policy` column on the knowledge store — `always` (every turn, what
  `domain="hot"` has always meant), `retrieved` (on a relevant query; the default) or
  `on_demand` (only through `memory_recall`) — rides `memory_ingest` / `memory_recall` /
  `memory_list`, the console's knowledge rows and `sdk.knowledge_add`. A hot write is
  stamped `always` automatically, and the `knowledge.hot_write_confirm` gate now refuses
  `delivery_policy="always"` on any domain, not just `domain="hot"`. On the first open after
  upgrading, existing rows are classified once from their `domain` into `memory_kind` and
  `delivery_policy` (#3205 added the typed columns but left every row untyped); the pass
  only fills empty cells and is recorded so it never re-runs. Delivery itself is unchanged
  until D6 switches the always-on reader to the new column.
