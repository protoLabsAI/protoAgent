The CLI guide gathers the deck in one place — screens and keys, credentials, offline mode, `--all`, the desktop binary — ADR 0075 carries the amendment that supersedes "chat is `proto`'s job" for the fleet and resolves its open question 2 (live hub first, badged disk fallback), ADR 0042 cross-references it, and the README's capability map has a row for the deck.
