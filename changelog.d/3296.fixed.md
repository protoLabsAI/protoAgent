**Creating a sister agent no longer dead-ends on a shared OAuth login.** The guard that
merges a legacy per-instance credential store into the machine-wide one compared the two
files byte-for-byte — but OAuth stores rotate on every refresh, so two copies of the *same*
account stop matching within minutes and the create failed with "the box already has a
different login", pointing at a remedy the console can't perform. It now compares the
ChatGPT account id: same account deduplicates onto the fresher store (a Codex refresh token
is single-use, so the older copy may already be burned), and a genuinely different — or
unidentifiable — account is reported as a warning on a successful create instead of killing it.
