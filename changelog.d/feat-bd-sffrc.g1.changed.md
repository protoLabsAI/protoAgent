- **Jobs and stopped-goal recovery in Work (#5100).** Former tasks now read as a **Job**: a not-started one (open or deferred) shows a dashed-circle icon, the meta "not started · added <when>", and **Start now** / **Close job** — Start now moves it from Coming up to Doing now through the shared source refresh, Close job closes it out of Work, and a failed action keeps the row actionable with a warning. A started blocked job stays in Doing now and says "blocked"; a closed job leaves the list. A goal that stops short of its finish line reads neutral **Goal stopped** in Needs you (never an error) with **Keep going** and **Drop the goal**; keeping it going returns it to Doing now, dropping it or meeting the finish line removes it from Work. Goal details lead with the finish line and read "step N of M · checking after each turn".
