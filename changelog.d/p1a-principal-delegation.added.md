- **Human-operator delegation across the proxy and loopback.** The fleet proxy strips untrusted principal headers and mints a short-lived signed delegation bound to one member, method, context and operation; the owner verifies issuer, audience, expiry, nonce and revocation generation, failing closed when revocation can't be read. The loopback self-POST carries a single-operation delegation redeemed idempotently. A swapped fleet bearer stays a peer, and localhost and self-invocations never confer human-operator status.
