- **A scheduled retry no longer ends the turn with a silent "I'll pick this back up" (#4651).**
  When `schedule_task` (one-shot) or `wait` succeeds — often right after a tool call failed
  (e.g. image generation hitting a gateway 524) — their success results now carry a line of
  guidance telling the model to say, before it ends the turn, what happened and what will run
  when the schedule fires, instead of yielding on a bare "I'll pick this back up in 5 minutes"
  that leaves the user unaware anything went wrong. Recurring (cron) schedules get the lighter
  "say what was scheduled" wording. Scheduling behaviour is unchanged.
