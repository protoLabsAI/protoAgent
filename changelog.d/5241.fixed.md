- **A late-delivered inbox item now says how old it is, so the agent can weigh it against newer instructions (#5241).** When the now-fire delivers an item queued more than an hour ago — the restart recovery drain can surface `next`/`later` pages more than a day old — it prefixes the message with one plain line naming the queued time (UTC), how many hours late the delivery is, and that newer instructions may supersede it, then a blank line and the original text unchanged. Items within the threshold, and any with a missing or unparseable `created_at`, are delivered byte-for-byte unchanged. The message's `created_at` now rides in the A2A metadata for every item, and the prefix leaves the dedup identity (`messageId`/`contextId`) untouched. Previously a day-old item was delivered verbatim and read as a current instruction, which on one agent made it cancel a newer standing schedule that had already replaced the old arrangement.
