- **Fleet read tools for the hub agent.** With `tools.fleet_diagnostics.enabled` on, the hub agent gets two read-only tools: `fleet_status` (per member: running, healthy, version, model, last health change, counts of pending decisions and waiting tasks) and `fleet_pending` (each member's pending decisions and waiting tasks). Status refreshes the supervisor health record through its existing sweep before reading the roster, so health and its last-change timestamp are available without a background loop; a failed refresh reports unknown. Both tools use the shared member read, apply the `fleet_diagnostics` redaction and bounds, report an unreadable member as "unreachable", and are not bound inside a workspace member or for federation and public callers. No new config key. Refs #5534.
