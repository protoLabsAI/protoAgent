- **Talk to a fleet member from the deck (#3469).** `enter` on an online member opens a conversation: your messages and the member's answers stream in the transcript, every tool call of the turn appears in a WORK tree as it happens — a subagent's own calls nested under its `task` card, with args, result, duration, and the true result size; `enter` on a card opens the full args and result — thinking folds behind a one-line count, and the turn's cost lands at the end. `esc` cancels a running turn; `ctrl+n` starts a new session, `ctrl+s` lists the member's console sessions and replays one with its tool cards. Sessions use the console's own id shape, so a terminal conversation is waiting in the browser and vice versa. After 45 s of silence the durable task is consulted: a turn still working is re-attached to (`SubscribeToTask`), a finished one is finalized from the record — never fabricated. Under the hood: a Python A2A client (1.0 wire) and one frame reducer ported from the console's, shared by live streams, resubscribe, and replay. Third slice of the fleet deck (epic #3466).
