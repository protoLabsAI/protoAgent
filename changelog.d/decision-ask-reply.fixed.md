- **An agent's reply after asking a decision is its own again.** Since v0.261.1 every turn that asked a decision ended on the same line, "I've asked you this; answer whenever you're ready.", in place of the agent's real reply, however much work the turn had done. The agent now writes the turn's last message itself (what it did and what it is asking), and the card still shows the question. If it writes nothing, the fallback names the question instead of repeating one fixed line.
