- **A turn that ends on `wait` now tells you what it did and what it's coming back for.** It used to reply only "I'll pick this back up in 2 minutes 30 seconds.", with nothing on what the turn had done or what the agent would pick back up. `wait` now takes a required `summary`, one or two sentences written to the user, and a turn with no other text replies "<summary> I'll pick this back up in <duration>." The agent's note to itself (`then`) still stays out of the reply and still shows on the wait card.
