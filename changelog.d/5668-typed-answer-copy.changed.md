- **Decision cards say when you answered by typing.** A decision answered with a typed reply
  now reads "You replied in chat" in the chat transcript, and "Already answered in chat" with
  the reply (cut at 80 characters) in the Decisions panel, instead of quoting the reply as a
  chosen option. While a typed reply is on its way, the card shows "Sending your reply…" in
  place of the options. Decisions answered by picking an option, and older records, read
  "You chose" as before. Refs #5668.
