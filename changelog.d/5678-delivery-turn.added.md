- **The agent receives a saved decision answer as an ordinary turn.** When the answer route starts a turn to deliver an answer to a decision that never paused the chat, the chat driver now runs it as a normal new turn on the decision's thread instead of rejecting it as a stale resume. The agent reads the answer from the decisions ledger, never from the message text. The turn is refused, and no turn starts, when the decision is still pending, was already delivered, or belongs to another chat. The driver records the delivery when it takes the answer, so a turn queued behind a busy chat is not mistaken for a duplicate. Decisions that paused the chat resume exactly as before. Nothing creates these decisions until `decisions.never_block` is turned on in a later slice. Refs #5678.
