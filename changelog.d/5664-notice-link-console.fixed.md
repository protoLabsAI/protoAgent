- **Tapping a notice that is not a decision opens the page it is about, even with Home off.** A fleet-member-down or spend-limit push notice used to leave the console where it was unless Home held its entry. The console now asks the hub once about the notice and either hands the tap to the console of the agent that raised it, or opens the console page the notice links to (same origin, under `/app/` only). Decisions still open their answer form, and Home's entry still wins when Home holds one. Every outcome is written to the tap log. Refs #5664.
